Sessions

Complete sessions built by energy tier. Pick the one that matches how you actually feel this morning.

8 entries. Each page states whether its numbers are SOURCED or ESTIMATED.

  • Low-Energy 20-Minute SessionA composed session of 4 LOW demand_tier exercises, total time ~20 min including rests; structure derived from the EXERCISE rows in the matrix
  • Moderate 30-Minute SessionA composed session of 2 MODERATE + 2 LOW demand_tier exercises, ~30 min; selection algorithm picks from the EXERCISE rows by demand_tier
  • High-Energy 40-Minute SessionA composed session of 4 MODERATE exercises, ~40 min; only scheduled on days the user logs HIGH energy for two consecutive mornings
  • Deload Week PlanA structured week of LOW demand_tier sessions only, 4 days, ~20 min each; every exercise uses its deload_variant; explicitly framed as part of the program, not a failure
  • Upper Body Focus 30-Minute SessionA composed session of 4 upper-body exercises; ~30 min.
  • Lower Body Focus 30-Minute SessionA composed session of 4 lower-body exercises; ~30 min.
  • Core and Mobility 20-Minute SessionA composed session of floor core work and gentle stretching; ~20 min.
  • Hotel Room 20-Minute SessionA composed session of 4 bodyweight exercises needing zero equipment; ~20 min.

How this page was built. Every number is either linked to a public source (FDA labels, USDA FoodData Central, published trials, ACSM/NHS guidance) or labeled ESTIMATED with its method stated on the page. Data last checked: 2025-09-15.
