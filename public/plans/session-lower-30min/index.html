Lower Body Focus 30-Minute Session

One decision, then execute: a composed session of 4 lower-body exercises; ~30 min. Energy cost moderate; fallback drop to chair sits and calf raises if knees feel stiff. The exercises inside are linked below, each with its own lighter swap.

If / then: choosing today's version

  • If your morning log reads LOW: run Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.

Until the logging tool ships, a one-tap note on your phone counts as a log.

How to run it today

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When to skip it

Do not run this session if your last two morning logs read LOW — take the deload week instead and come back refreshed.

Check with a clinician if

Sharp knee or hip pain; stop and consult a clinician.
