Deload Week Plan

The whole session in one paragraph: a structured week of LOW demand_tier sessions only, 4 days, ~20 min each; every exercise uses its deload_variant; explicitly framed as part of the program, not a failure. Mid-session downgrade, pre-decided: if any session still feels too much, drop to just walking. Equipment needed: bodyweight. Total commitment is the time listed, rests included.

Pick today's version

  • If your morning log reads LOW: run Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  • If your morning log reads MODERATE: run Moderate 30-Minute Session.
  • If you have logged HIGH two mornings in a row: run High-Energy 40-Minute Session.

Until the logging tool ships, a one-tap note on your phone counts as a log.

Your session, step by step

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When this is the wrong choice

Do not run this session if your last two morning logs read LOW — take the deload week instead and come back refreshed.

Stop signals

None; a deload is the safe option
