GLP-1 Receptor Agonist

GLP-1 Receptor Agonist in a sentence: a class of medication that mimics the incretin hormone GLP-1; originally for type 2 diabetes, now licensed for chronic weight management. In practice, watch for: confusing brand names with active ingredients — Wegovy and Ozempic are both semaglutide; Zepbound and Mounjaro are both tirzepatide. The source for this definition is linked at the foot of the page.
