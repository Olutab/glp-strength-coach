Early Satiety

Early Satiety in a sentence: the physiological sensation of fullness after a smaller-than-usual amount of food; a direct effect of slowed gastric emptying from GLP-1 receptor agonists. In practice, watch for: thinking it means you should eat even less — early satiety is a signal to eat smaller, more often, not to skip meals.
