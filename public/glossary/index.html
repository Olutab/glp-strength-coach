Glossary

Plain-English definitions of every term used across this site, each linked to a public source.

11 entries. Each page states whether its numbers are SOURCED or ESTIMATED.

  • DeloadA planned reduction in training volume or intensity, usually lasting one week; explicitly normalised here as a weekly feature, not a failure event
  • Muscle PreservationThe goal of training while in a caloric deficit on GLP-1; loss of lean mass accounts for ~30–40% of weight lost in GLP-1 trials without resistance training
  • Early SatietyThe physiological sensation of fullness after a smaller-than-usual amount of food; a direct effect of slowed gastric emptying from GLP-1 receptor agonists
  • GLP-1 Receptor AgonistA class of medication that mimics the incretin hormone GLP-1; originally for type 2 diabetes, now licensed for chronic weight management
  • Protein Per 100 kcalA ranking metric derived by dividing grams of protein by the calorie content of a food per 100 kcal; higher values indicate more protein for less appetite budget
  • RIR (Reps in Reserve)How many more reps you could have done before failure; 2 RIR means you stopped with 2 left in the tank.
  • Progressive OverloadGradually increasing the challenge to the muscles over time to force adaptation.
  • TitrationThe process of slowly increasing a medication dose over weeks to minimise side effects.
  • Lean MassThe weight of everything in your body except fat (muscle, bone, water, organs).
  • Metabolic AdaptationThe body's process of slowing down calorie burn in response to prolonged dieting.
  • SarcopeniaThe involuntary loss of skeletal muscle mass and strength, common in adults over 50.

Limits of this page. Training and food guidance for adults over 50 using GLP-1 medications. Not medical advice, and it does not predict results. Medication decisions belong to your prescriber. Stop and contact a clinician for chest pain, fainting, persistent vomiting, or rapid unintentional weight loss.

How this page was built. Every number is either linked to a public source (FDA labels, USDA FoodData Central, published trials, ACSM/NHS guidance) or labeled ESTIMATED with its method stated on the page. Data last checked: 2025-09-15.
