Nausea Day Playbook

Today's plan in one breath: common in the first 4–12 weeks of GLP-1 escalation; nausea reported in ~20–40% of STEP trial participants. The default move is skip the session entirely and do a 10-minute walk; this is data, not failure. A low-energy day is information about the medication working, not a character flaw, and the session simply shrinks to match it.

A plan for the day

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Skip the session entirely and do a 10-minute walk; this is data, not failure
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Whey Protein Isolate, Plain.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

When this is the wrong choice

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: vomiting that prevents keeping fluids down; severe abdominal pain — contact your clinician.

Stop signals

Vomiting that prevents keeping fluids down; severe abdominal pain — contact your clinician
