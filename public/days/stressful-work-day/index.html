Stressful Work Day

Stressful Work Day happens to most people on a GLP-1 at some point. What we know: high mental fatigue feels like physical fatigue; requires a mental break, not a hard workout. What to do: do 10 minutes of deep breathing and the Bird-Dog exercise to reset. Decide once, write it down, and stop re-litigating it every morning.

A plan for the day

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Do 10 minutes of deep breathing and the Bird-Dog exercise to reset.
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Bird-Dog.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

When this is the wrong choice

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: contact a clinician.
