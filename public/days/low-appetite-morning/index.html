Low-Appetite Morning

Read this first on a hard morning: appetite is typically lowest in the morning for GLP-1 users, especially in the first 8 weeks of escalation. The programme's answer: skip the large breakfast and instead take a 30g whey shake mid-morning. Nothing about this day cancels your training; it resizes it.

What today looks like

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Skip the large breakfast and instead take a 30g whey shake mid-morning
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Whey Protein Isolate, Plain.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

Not the right tool if

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: skipping food for more than 24 hours; dizziness — contact your clinician.

When to stop and check with a clinician
