Sleep-Deprived Morning

Read this first on a hard morning: poor sleep increases injury risk and reduces glucose tolerance; requires caution. The programme's answer: downgrade to the Low-Energy 20-Minute Session; skip heavy leg work. Nothing about this day cancels your training; it resizes it.

What today looks like

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Downgrade to the Low-Energy 20-Minute Session; skip heavy leg work.
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

Not the right tool if

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: extreme dizziness or falling asleep during the session; stop and rest..

When to stop and check with a clinician

Extreme dizziness or falling asleep during the session; stop and rest.
