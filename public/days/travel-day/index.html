Travel Day

When travel Day shows up, the question is not "push or quit" — it is "which version of today?" Facts: sitting for long periods increases stiffness and DVT risk; requires mobility. Default: do ankle pumps and calf raises in your seat every hour. The schedule below turns that into a day you can actually run.

A plan for the day

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Do ankle pumps and calf raises in your seat every hour.
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Standing Calf Raise.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

When this is the wrong choice

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: calf pain, swelling, or shortness of breath; seek emergency medical care..

Stop signals

Calf pain, swelling, or shortness of breath; seek emergency medical care.
