Injection-Day Fatigue

Read this first on a hard morning: many users report lower energy 12–48h after the weekly dose; anecdotal pattern across GLP-1 forums and trial safety sections. The programme's answer: schedule injection day as a deload day automatically every week. Nothing about this day cancels your training; it resizes it.

Hour by hour

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Schedule injection day as a deload day automatically every week
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Deload Week Plan.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

When to skip it

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: fatigue lasting more than 3 days; fainting — contact your clinician.
