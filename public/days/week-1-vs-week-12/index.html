Week 1 vs Week 12 on GLP-1

The short version for a rough day: appetite, energy and tolerance shift significantly across the 12–20 week escalation schedule; training intensity should follow the dose, not the calendar. Planned response: treat weeks 1–4 as pure habit-building, not strength-building. Consistency on this medication is built from adjustable sessions, not heroic ones.

Hour by hour

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Treat weeks 1–4 as pure habit-building, not strength-building
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Deload Week Plan.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

When to skip it

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: severe or persistent side-effects — contact your prescribing clinician before training.
