Cold or Flu Day

Today's plan in one breath: training with a fever or chest infection is dangerous; requires complete rest. The default move is zero training. Focus on hydration and sleep until fever is gone for 24h. A low-energy day is information about the medication working, not a character flaw, and the session simply shrinks to match it.

What today looks like

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Zero training. Focus on hydration and sleep until fever is gone for 24h.
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Deload Week Plan.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

Not the right tool if

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: fever over 38C (100.4F), chest pain, or shortness of breath; see a doctor..

When to stop and check with a clinician

Fever over 38C (100.4F), chest pain, or shortness of breath; see a doctor.
