Early Satiety Day

Read this first on a hard morning: a core GLP-1 effect; gastric emptying slows significantly, so small portions feel like large meals. The programme's answer: eat protein first, before vegetables or carbs, to secure the macro target. Nothing about this day cancels your training; it resizes it.

What today looks like

  1. Morning. Log energy before deciding anything. If LOW, today's session is already chosen: Low-Energy 20-Minute Session.
  2. Training window. Eat protein first, before vegetables or carbs, to secure the macro target
  3. Food anchor. Protein first, small portion: Cottage Cheese, Low-Fat.
  4. Evening. Hydrate, note one line in the log (energy 1–5, session done yes/no). Two data points beat two hours of worry.

Not the right tool if

This playbook is the wrong tool if symptoms escalate: unintentional rapid weight loss; inability to keep any food down — contact your clinician.

When to stop and check with a clinician

Unintentional rapid weight loss; inability to keep any food down — contact your clinician
