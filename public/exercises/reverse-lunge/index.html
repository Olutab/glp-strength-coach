Reverse Lunge

Reverse Lunge earns its slot because it delivers a strength stimulus without demanding a good day. In practice: unilateral leg strength with less knee shear than forward lunges; 3×6 per side. Cost: moderate (save it for days when you feel reasonably good). Rough-day answer, already decided: reduce to a supported split squat with the back heel on a low book.

Running it well

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

Not the right tool if

Swap Reverse Lunge out today if knees and hips; the named deload exists for exactly this.

Where people go wrong

Letting the front knee cave inward — keep the knee over the second toe

When to stop and check with a clinician

Knee or hip pain; loss of balance stepping back
