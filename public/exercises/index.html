Exercises

Every movement here uses bodyweight or light dumbbells, and every one has a named low-energy swap. Nothing on this site is "push through it".

22 entries. Each page states whether its numbers are SOURCED or ESTIMATED.

  • Wall Push-UpVertical pressing pattern with minimal shoulder load; 3×10–12
