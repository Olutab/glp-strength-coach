Seated Bicep Curl

The honest summary of Seated Bicep Curl: arm flexion from a seated position to prevent swinging; 3×10. We rate it low for energy, so it fits almost any day, including a rough one. The deload is named on purpose: use a lighter resistance band instead of dumbbells. Planning the easier version before you need it is what keeps training weeks connected.

The quick read

Also known aschair bicep curl

Best forfirst-time lifters, returning after a break
Low-energy swapUse a lighter resistance band instead of dumbbells.
Joint caution (50+)Elbows; keep the elbows pinned to the ribs.

Running it well

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

Not the right tool if

Swap Seated Bicep Curl out today if elbows; the named deload exists for exactly this.

Where people go wrong

Swinging the torso to lift the weight; sit tall and isolate the arm.

When to stop and check with a clinician

Elbow joint pain or wrist strain.
