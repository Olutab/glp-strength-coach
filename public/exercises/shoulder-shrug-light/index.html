Light Dumbbell Shoulder Shrug

Light Dumbbell Shoulder Shrug is a low-energy movement: upper trapezius activation holding light weights; 3×15. On a morning when the log says low, the planned swap is drop the weights and shrug with just bodyweight — written in advance so there is nothing to decide when you feel rough. That swap is part of the programme, not a consolation prize.

Key facts

Joint caution (50+)Neck; do not roll the shoulders, just lift straight up and down.

Your session, step by step

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When this is the wrong choice

Swap Light Dumbbell Shoulder Shrug out today if neck; the named deload exists for exactly this.

The common trap

Rolling the shoulders in circles; this grinds the shoulder joint.

Stop signals

Neck stiffness or headache from tensing the neck.
