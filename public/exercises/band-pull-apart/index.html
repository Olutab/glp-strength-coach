Band Pull-Apart

The honest summary of Band Pull-Apart: rear-delt and upper-back activation; 3×12. We rate it low for energy, so it fits almost any day, including a rough one. The deload is named on purpose: use a lighter band or reduce the range. Planning the easier version before you need it is what keeps training weeks connected.

Running it well

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

Not the right tool if

Swap Band Pull-Apart out today if shoulders; the named deload exists for exactly this.

Where people go wrong

Shrugging the shoulders up toward the ears

When to stop and check with a clinician

Shoulder pinching; wrist strain from gripping
