Bird-Dog

If you read one paragraph, make it this: anti-rotation core stabiliser on all fours; 3×6 per side. Energy cost low — it fits almost any day, including a rough one; on a low day, knees only — lift just one limb at a time. Everything further down the page is detail; this is the whole decision.

Your session, step by step

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When this is the wrong choice

Swap Bird-Dog out today if lower back; the named deload exists for exactly this.

The common trap

Reaching high instead of long — reach fingertip to toe in a straight line

Stop signals

Sharp low-back pain; wrist discomfort on the floor
