Light Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift

The honest summary of Light Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift: hip hinge holding light dumbbells in front of thighs; 3×10 typical. We rate it moderate for energy, so save it for days when you feel reasonably good. The deload is named on purpose: drop the weights and perform an unweighted hip hinge to a chair. Planning the easier version before you need it is what keeps training weeks connected.

Key facts

Also known asRDL, hamstring hinge
Energy costModerate — needs a decent day
Role in the planStrength stimulus
EquipmentLight dumbbells
Best forreturning after a break, trained adults over 50
Low-energy swapDrop the weights and perform an unweighted hip hinge to a chair.
Joint caution (50+)Lower back; keep the back flat and hinge only as far as hamstring flexibility allows.

Your session, step by step

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When this is the wrong choice

Swap Light Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift out today if lower back; the named deload exists for exactly this.

The common trap

Rounding the lower back to reach the weights lower.

Stop signals

Sharp lower back pain or tingling down the legs; stop and consult a clinician.
