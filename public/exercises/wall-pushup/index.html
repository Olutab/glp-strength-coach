Wall Push-Up

The honest summary of Wall Push-Up: vertical pressing pattern with minimal shoulder load; 3×10–12. We rate it low for energy, so it fits almost any day, including a rough one. The deload is named on purpose: reduce range by stepping 15cm closer to the wall. Planning the easier version before you need it is what keeps training weeks connected.

Running it well

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

Not the right tool if

Swap Wall Push-Up out today if wrists; the named deload exists for exactly this.

Where people go wrong

Pushing the head forward — keep the ears between the upper arms

When to stop and check with a clinician

Shoulder pinching at the top of the movement
