Standing Band Reverse Fly

Standing Band Reverse Fly is a low-energy movement: upper back and posture correction using a band; 3×12. On a morning when the log says low, the planned swap is use a lighter band or reduce the range of motion — written in advance so there is nothing to decide when you feel rough. That swap is part of the programme, not a consolation prize.

How to run it today

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When to skip it

Swap Standing Band Reverse Fly out today if shoulders; the named deload exists for exactly this.

The mistake to avoid

Shrugging the shoulders up to the ears during the pull.

Check with a clinician if

Shoulder pinching or upper back spasm.
