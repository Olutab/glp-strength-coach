Goblet Squat

If you read one paragraph, make it this: hip-hinge squat holding a light dumbbell at chest height; 3×8–10 typical. Energy cost moderate — save it for days when you feel reasonably good; on a low day, drop to bodyweight box squat to a chair; halve the sets. Everything further down the page is detail; this is the whole decision.

How to run it today

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When to skip it

Swap Goblet Squat out today if rising from the bottom can stress stiff knees; the named deload exists for exactly this.

The mistake to avoid

Going too heavy before the hip hinge is automatic — the load should feel easy for the first 2 weeks

Check with a clinician if

Dizziness when standing up from the bottom; sharp knee pain at depth
