Wall Sit

Wall Sit earns its slot because it delivers a safety guardrail without demanding a good day. In practice: isometric leg endurance against a wall; 3×20s. Cost: low (it fits almost any day, including a rough one). Rough-day answer, already decided: slide higher up the wall to reduce the knee bend.

Key facts

Also known aswall squat hold
Energy costLow — fine on a rough day
Role in the planSafety guardrail
EquipmentBodyweight
Best forfirst-time lifters
Low-energy swapSlide higher up the wall to reduce the knee bend.
Joint caution (50+)Knees; do not let the knees cave inward or extend past the toes.

Your session, step by step

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When this is the wrong choice

Swap Wall Sit out today if knees; the named deload exists for exactly this.

The common trap

Holding the breath; breathe steadily throughout the hold.

Stop signals

Sharp knee pain or dizziness from holding breath.
