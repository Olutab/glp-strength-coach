Dead Bug

The honest summary of Dead Bug: supine core stabiliser; 3×5 per side. We rate it low for energy, so it fits almost any day, including a rough one. The deload is named on purpose: keep both feet on the floor; reach only one arm. Planning the easier version before you need it is what keeps training weeks connected.

Your session, step by step

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When this is the wrong choice

Swap Dead Bug out today if lower back; the named deload exists for exactly this.

The common trap

Lifting the lower back off the floor to reach further

Stop signals

Lower-back arching or pain; neck strain
