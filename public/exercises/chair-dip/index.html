Chair Dip

The honest summary of Chair Dip: tricep extension using a sturdy chair; 3×8. We rate it moderate for energy, so save it for days when you feel reasonably good. The deload is named on purpose: reduce the range of motion to just a 5cm bend. Planning the easier version before you need it is what keeps training weeks connected.

How to run it today

Planning estimate (labeled, not measured): budget about 7 minutes per movement including 60–90 s rests, so three sets land inside a 20–30 minute session. Structure listed on this page: undefined. If the clock or your energy runs out early, stop after the last completed set — a partial session still logs.

When to skip it

Swap Chair Dip out today if shoulders and wrists; the named deload exists for exactly this.

The mistake to avoid

Leaning too far forward; keep the back close to the chair.

Check with a clinician if

Sharp shoulder pain or wrist strain.
