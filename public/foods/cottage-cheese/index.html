Cottage Cheese, Low-Fat

Cottage Cheese, Low-Fat answers one question: how much protein can I bank for the least appetite spend? Answer: slow-digesting casein protein, easy on suppressed appetite; ~11g protein per 100 kcal. Low-appetite version: a 60g snack portion rather than a full bowl. The source link below shows exactly where the number comes from.

The quick read

Also known aspaneer, curd cheese
Energy costLow — fine on a rough day

Low-energy swapA 60g snack portion rather than a full bowl

What it costs your appetite

Worked example, using this page's own number (11 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 22 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 37% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

Not the right tool if

Skip Cottage Cheese, Low-Fat today if none; stop only if dairy causes bloating or discomfort; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

Where people go wrong

Pairing with high-fat crackers that slow gastric emptying further
