Chia Seeds, Dry

One paragraph, no fluff: fibre and protein that expand in liquid; ~16g protein per 100g. When the plate looks too big, soak 1 tablespoon in water or almond milk to make a small pudding. Everything else on this page — pairings, mistakes, comparisons — supports that single trade-off between protein and appetite.

What it costs your appetite

Worked example, using this page's own number (16 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 32 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 53% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

Not the right tool if

Skip Chia Seeds, Dry today if none; stop if severe bloating occurs.; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

Where people go wrong

Eating them dry without enough liquid, which can cause choking or blockages.
