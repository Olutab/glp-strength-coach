Lentils, Cooked

One paragraph, no fluff: plant protein plus slow carbs; ~9g protein per 100 kcal. When the plate looks too big, a small 80g side portion. Everything else on this page — pairings, mistakes, comparisons — supports that single trade-off between protein and appetite.

Key facts

Also known asbrown or green lentils
Energy costModerate — needs a decent day
Role in the planProtein delivery
Best forreturning after a break
Low-energy swapA small 80g side portion

Portion math

Worked example, using this page's own number (9 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 18 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 30% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When this is the wrong choice

Skip Lentils, Cooked today if none; reduce portion if bloating occurs; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The common trap

Treating it as purely protein — it also delivers carbs and fibre
