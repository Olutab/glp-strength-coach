Peanut Butter Powder

The quick read on Peanut Butter Powder: peanut flavour with most of the fat removed; ~50g protein per 100g. On days when eating feels like work, mix 2 tablespoons with water to make a low-calorie paste. Protein per calorie is the metric that matters when the appetite budget is small, and this page ranks it honestly.

Key facts

Also known asPB2, defatted peanut flour
Energy costLow — fine on a rough day

Portion math

Worked example, using this page's own number (50 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 100 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 100% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When this is the wrong choice

Skip Peanut Butter Powder today if it disagrees with you; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The common trap

Mixing it with regular peanut butter; that defeats the low-calorie purpose.
