Canned Black Beans, Rinsed

One paragraph, no fluff: plant protein plus high fibre; ~8g protein per 100 kcal. When the plate looks too big, a small 60g side portion to add bulk to a meal. Everything else on this page — pairings, mistakes, comparisons — supports that single trade-off between protein and appetite.

Portion math

Worked example, using this page's own number (8 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 16 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 27% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When this is the wrong choice

Skip Canned Black Beans, Rinsed today if none; stop if severe gas or bloating occurs.; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The common trap

Not rinsing them; rinsing removes excess sodium and gas-causing compounds.
