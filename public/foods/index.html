Foods

Protein-first foods ranked by how much protein they deliver per calorie — because on a GLP-1 your appetite budget is small.

23 entries. Each page states whether its numbers are SOURCED or ESTIMATED.

  • Greek Yogurt, Plain Non-FatHigh protein per calorie with minimal appetite load; ~10g protein per 100 kcal
  • Cottage Cheese, Low-FatSlow-digesting casein protein, easy on suppressed appetite; ~11g protein per 100 kcal
  • Canned Tuna in Water, DrainedLean protein with negligible fat; ~21g protein per 100 kcal
  • Cooked Skinless Chicken BreastHighest protein-per-calorie whole food; ~31g protein per 100 kcal
  • Edamame, Shelled and CookedPlant protein with fibre; ~11g protein per 100 kcal
  • Tempeh, CookedFirm plant protein with gut-friendly fermentation; ~20g protein per 100 kcal
  • Lentils, CookedPlant protein plus slow carbs; ~9g protein per 100 kcal
  • Firm Tofu, RawNeutral plant protein base; ~8g protein per 100 kcal
  • Sardines Canned in WaterProtein plus omega-3 and calcium from bones; ~25g protein per 100 kcal
  • Whey Protein Isolate, PlainConcentrated protein with almost zero appetite load; ~90g protein per 100g powder
  • Whole Egg, BoiledComplete protein with fat and micronutrients; ~13g protein per 100 kcal
  • Shrimp, CookedExtremely lean protein with almost no fat; ~20g protein per 100 kcal.
  • Deli Turkey Breast, Low SodiumConvenient lean protein; ~18g protein per 100 kcal.
  • Hemp Seeds, ShelledPlant protein with omega-3s; ~31g protein per 100g (but calorie dense).
  • Chia Seeds, DryFibre and protein that expand in liquid; ~16g protein per 100g.
  • Nutritional YeastCheesy-tasting plant protein and B-vitamin source; ~50g protein per 100g.
  • Canned Black Beans, RinsedPlant protein plus high fibre; ~8g protein per 100 kcal.
  • Seitan, CookedExtremely dense plant protein; ~25g protein per 100 kcal.
  • Pumpkin Seeds, ShelledPlant protein with magnesium and zinc; ~30g protein per 100g.
  • Raw AlmondsCrunchy plant protein with healthy fats; ~21g protein per 100g.
  • Peanut Butter PowderPeanut flavour with most of the fat removed; ~50g protein per 100g.
  • Baked Salmon FilletProtein plus high omega-3s for joint and heart health; ~20g protein per 100g.
  • Low-Sugar Protein BarConvenient emergency protein; typically 20g protein for ~200 kcal.

Limits of this page. Training and food guidance for adults over 50 using GLP-1 medications. Not medical advice, and it does not predict results. Medication decisions belong to your prescriber. Stop and contact a clinician for chest pain, fainting, persistent vomiting, or rapid unintentional weight loss.

How this page was built. Every number is either linked to a public source (FDA labels, USDA FoodData Central, published trials, ACSM/NHS guidance) or labeled ESTIMATED with its method stated on the page. Data last checked: 2025-09-15.
