Canned Tuna in Water, Drained

Canned Tuna in Water, Drained is protein first: lean protein with negligible fat; ~21g protein per 100 kcal. When appetite is small, the move is half a can (~55g) as a snack. Nothing here promises outcomes; the goal is hitting protein with the least appetite spend on a day the medication has already capped.

The protein math

Worked example, using this page's own number (21 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 42 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 70% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When to skip it

Skip Canned Tuna in Water, Drained today if none; limit albacore due to mercury; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The mistake to avoid

Eating more than 3 cans per week due to mercury; stick to light tuna and limit albacore
