Whole Egg, Boiled

Whole Egg, Boiled answers one question: how much protein can I bank for the least appetite spend? Answer: complete protein with fat and micronutrients; ~13g protein per 100 kcal. Low-appetite version: one egg as a snack when appetite is minimal. The source link below shows exactly where the number comes from.

The protein math

Worked example, using this page's own number (13 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 26 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 43% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When to skip it

Skip Whole Egg, Boiled today if none; stop only if you have a known egg allergy; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The mistake to avoid

Skipping the yolk — it contains half the protein and most of the micronutrients
