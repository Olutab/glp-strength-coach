Greek Yogurt, Plain Non-Fat

Start with the number that matters on a GLP-1: high protein per calorie with minimal appetite load; ~10g protein per 100 kcal. Portion strategy for a low-appetite day: swap to a smaller 80g serving or a spoonful of skyr in tea. Treat the figure as a planning anchor, sourced below, and adjust to what your prescriber and your body agree on.

The protein math

Worked example, using this page's own number (10 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 20 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 33% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When to skip it

Skip Greek Yogurt, Plain Non-Fat today if none; stop only if dairy causes bloating or discomfort; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The mistake to avoid

Adding sugary granola that fills the appetite budget without much protein
