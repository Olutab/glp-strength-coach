Low-Sugar Protein Bar

Low-Sugar Protein Bar answers one question: how much protein can I bank for the least appetite spend? Answer: convenient emergency protein; typically 20g protein for ~200 kcal. Low-appetite version: keep one in your bag for days when you cannot prepare food. The source link below shows exactly where the number comes from.

The protein math

Worked example, using this page's own number (20 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 40 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 67% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When to skip it

Skip Low-Sugar Protein Bar today if none; stop if sugar alcohols cause severe bloating.; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The mistake to avoid

Choosing bars with 10g of sugar alcohols which can worsen GLP-1 GI issues.
