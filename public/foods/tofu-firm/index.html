Firm Tofu, Raw

Firm Tofu, Raw is protein first: neutral plant protein base; ~8g protein per 100 kcal. When appetite is small, the move is crumble 60g into a scramble. Nothing here promises outcomes; the goal is hitting protein with the least appetite spend on a day the medication has already capped.

Key facts

Role in the planProtein delivery
Best forreturning after a break, trained adults over 50
Low-energy swapCrumble 60g into a scramble

Portion math

Worked example, using this page's own number (8 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 16 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 27% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When this is the wrong choice

Skip Firm Tofu, Raw today if none; stop only if soy causes bloating; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The common trap

Pressing out too much water and leaving only a small amount — 150g raw yields ~12g protein
