Pumpkin Seeds, Shelled

Start with the number that matters on a GLP-1: plant protein with magnesium and zinc; ~30g protein per 100g. Portion strategy for a low-appetite day: sprinkle 1 tablespoon over a salad. Treat the figure as a planning anchor, sourced below, and adjust to what your prescriber and your body agree on.

Portion math

Worked example, using this page's own number (30 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 60 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 100% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When this is the wrong choice

Skip Pumpkin Seeds, Shelled today if it disagrees with you; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The common trap

Eating large handfuls; they are very high in fat and calories.
