Tempeh, Cooked

One paragraph, no fluff: firm plant protein with gut-friendly fermentation; ~20g protein per 100 kcal. When the plate looks too big, cube 40g into a stir-fry rather than eating as a steak. Everything else on this page — pairings, mistakes, comparisons — supports that single trade-off between protein and appetite.

Portion math

Worked example, using this page's own number (20 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 40 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 67% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When this is the wrong choice

Skip Tempeh, Cooked today if none; stop only if soy causes bloating; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The common trap

Skipping it because of texture — crumble it like mince if the slab form is off-putting
