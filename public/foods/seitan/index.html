Seitan, Cooked

Seitan, Cooked is protein first: extremely dense plant protein; ~25g protein per 100 kcal. When appetite is small, the move is cube 40g into a stir-fry. Nothing here promises outcomes; the goal is hitting protein with the least appetite spend on a day the medication has already capped.

At a glance

Also known aswheat meat, vital wheat gluten
Energy costModerate — needs a decent day
Role in the planProtein delivery
Best fortrained adults over 50
Low-energy swapCube 40g into a stir-fry.

The protein math

Worked example, using this page's own number (25 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 50 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 83% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When to skip it

Skip Seitan, Cooked today if none; absolutely avoid if you have celiac disease or gluten sensitivity.; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The mistake to avoid

Treating it as a whole food; it is highly processed and lacks micronutrients.
