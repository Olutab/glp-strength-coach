Edamame, Shelled and Cooked

Start with the number that matters on a GLP-1: plant protein with fibre; ~11g protein per 100 kcal. Portion strategy for a low-appetite day: a 50g side portion instead of a main. Treat the figure as a planning anchor, sourced below, and adjust to what your prescriber and your body agree on.

The protein math

Worked example, using this page's own number (11 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 22 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 37% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When to skip it

Skip Edamame, Shelled and Cooked today if none; stop only if soy causes bloating; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The mistake to avoid

Treating it as a free snack — it still counts toward the appetite budget
