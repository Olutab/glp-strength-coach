Baked Salmon Fillet

Start with the number that matters on a GLP-1: protein plus high omega-3s for joint and heart health; ~20g protein per 100g. Portion strategy for a low-appetite day: a small 80g portion as a main meal. Treat the figure as a planning anchor, sourced below, and adjust to what your prescriber and your body agree on.

What it costs your appetite

Worked example, using this page's own number (20 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 40 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 67% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

Not the right tool if

Skip Baked Salmon Fillet today if it disagrees with you; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

Where people go wrong

Overcooking it until dry; bake at a low temperature to keep it moist.
