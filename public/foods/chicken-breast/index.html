Cooked Skinless Chicken Breast

Start with the number that matters on a GLP-1: highest protein-per-calorie whole food; ~31g protein per 100 kcal. Portion strategy for a low-appetite day: shred a small amount into soup rather than eating as a slab. Treat the figure as a planning anchor, sourced below, and adjust to what your prescriber and your body agree on.

Portion math

Worked example, using this page's own number (31 g protein per reference serving): two reference servings give about 62 g of protein. Against a 60 g daily target split over three meals, that covers roughly 100% of the day in two small eats. The arithmetic is ours; the grams per serving come from the source below.

When this is the wrong choice

Skip Cooked Skinless Chicken Breast today if it disagrees with you; on a zero-appetite day a shake may beat any plate.

The common trap

Eating a whole breast when appetite is low — a 70g serving is enough
