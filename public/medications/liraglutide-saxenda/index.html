Liraglutide (Saxenda)

Liraglutide (Saxenda) sets the rhythm your training has to respect: daily subcutaneous GLP-1 receptor agonist; escalated to 3.0 mg daily. Concretely: because it is daily, there is no single 'injection day fatigue' spike. Treat side-effect windows as scheduling data, not as setbacks.
