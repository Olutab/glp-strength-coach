Semaglutide (Wegovy/Ozempic)

Semaglutide (Wegovy/Ozempic) sets the rhythm your training has to respect: weekly subcutaneous GLP-1 receptor agonist; typical 12–20 week escalation from 0.25 mg to 2.4 mg for Wegovy. Concretely: plan training intensity to match the escalation week, not the calendar week. Treat side-effect windows as scheduling data, not as setbacks.
