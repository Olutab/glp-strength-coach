Medications

Context pages for the medications themselves — what the escalation schedule means for how you train.

4 entries. Each page states whether its numbers are SOURCED or ESTIMATED.

  • Semaglutide (Wegovy/Ozempic)Weekly subcutaneous GLP-1 receptor agonist; typical 12–20 week escalation from 0.25 mg to 2.4 mg for Wegovy
  • Tirzepatide (Zepbound/Mounjaro)Weekly subcutaneous dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist; typical 12–20 week escalation from 2.5 mg to 15 mg for Zepbound
  • Liraglutide (Saxenda)Daily subcutaneous GLP-1 receptor agonist; escalated to 3.0 mg daily.
  • Oral Semaglutide (Rybelsus)Daily oral GLP-1 receptor agonist; must be taken on an empty stomach with plain water.

Limits of this page. Training and food guidance for adults over 50 using GLP-1 medications. Not medical advice, and it does not predict results. Medication decisions belong to your prescriber. Stop and contact a clinician for chest pain, fainting, persistent vomiting, or rapid unintentional weight loss.

How this page was built. Every number is either linked to a public source (FDA labels, USDA FoodData Central, published trials, ACSM/NHS guidance) or labeled ESTIMATED with its method stated on the page. Data last checked: 2025-09-15.
