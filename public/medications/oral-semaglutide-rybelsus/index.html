Oral Semaglutide (Rybelsus)

Short and useful: daily oral GLP-1 receptor agonist; must be taken on an empty stomach with plain water. Training implication: take it immediately upon waking, wait 30 mins before training or eating. If anything here seems to conflict with your prescriber's instructions, follow your prescriber.
