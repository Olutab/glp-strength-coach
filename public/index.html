Strength training that respects your medication

Most fitness sites assume you feel the same every day. On a GLP-1, you don't. Every page in this library starts from one question: given the energy you actually have today, what is the right amount? Low-energy days get a named, planned lighter option — a deload, not a failure.

Built for adults over 50 using bodyweight or light dumbbells, with protein guidance designed for a small appetite.

How this site stays honest

  • Every number is linked to a public source, or clearly labeled ESTIMATED with its method stated.
  • This is training guidance, not medical advice. Medication questions go to your prescriber.
  • No before/after promises, no outcome guarantees — the goal is muscle preservation and consistency.

Limits of this page. Training and food guidance for adults over 50 using GLP-1 medications. Not medical advice, and it does not predict results. Medication decisions belong to your prescriber. Stop and contact a clinician for chest pain, fainting, persistent vomiting, or rapid unintentional weight loss.
